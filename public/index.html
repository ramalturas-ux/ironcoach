Gout management
Gout flares from uric acid buildup. High purine foods = more uric acid. Fasting itself can temporarily spike uric acid — stay well hydrated.

Nitrotech Ripped + Gout
High protein intake (especially whey) can mildly elevate uric acid in some people. Monitor how you feel. If flares increase, consider reducing to 1 scoop/day and getting protein from chicken instead. Stay hydrated — target 3–4L water daily.
